## Less searching More listening

Finding an audio story you will enjoy can take repeated searches and trial and error. Language and genre are only part of the picture: atmosphere, storytelling style, and available time matter too.

We are building a personalized discovery website that brings those preferences together to help you choose your next story faster.

## Our vision

We want to become a trusted discovery companion for audio-story listeners, starting with one language and growing with our community.

Our goal is simple: help you feel understood while introducing you to stories and creators you might otherwise miss.

## How recommendations will work

1. Tell us your language, favorite stories, mood, and available time.
2. We match those preferences against a curated catalog enriched with AI-assisted story tags reviewed by a person.
3. You receive three suitable suggestions with explanations.
4. Your feedback helps improve later recommendations.

For the first version, AI helps prepare story information. The website filters and ranks the reviewed catalog in your browser. Preferences and feedback are saved on your device.

## How we plan to sustain the service

We plan to start with free story discovery. As the service grows, we may introduce optional premium discovery tools, clearly labeled editorial sponsorships, relevant affiliate partnerships, and services that help creators describe and categorize their stories.

These options are still being evaluated. Sponsored placements will be identified, and payment will not buy a position in organic recommendations. YouTube playback will remain freely accessible; optional paid services would cover our own additional discovery tools.

## Build requirements

- Include the problem, vision, and planned business model on the homepage.
- Keep monetization below the main discovery journey, under an accessible section titled How we plan to sustain the service.
- Describe planned features accurately before launch; update future-tense wording only after implementation.
- Link Find my next story to the preference flow and How recommendations work to its homepage section once those exist.
- Do not display unverified user counts, testimonials, recommendation accuracy, prices, or active paid plans.
- Review any paid feature and sponsorship implementation against YouTube policies before launch.
